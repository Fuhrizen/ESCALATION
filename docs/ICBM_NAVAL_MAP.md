# Naval weapons and geographic presentation reference

Reviewed 14 October 2026. This brief uses official publisher/developer material to guide ESCALATION. It records inspiration, not a claim of matching the reference game's undisclosed balance or implementation. No proprietary code, models, textures, screenshots or UI assets are imported.

## ESCALATION choices, not sourced parity

- **Weapon channels:** Every available channel owns its target-domain eligibility, range, cooldown and ammunition state. One unit may engage different valid threats through distinct channels. Exact simultaneous-fire rules, cooldowns, ammunition counts and kilometres are our tuned design; these diaries provide no verified numerical table.
- **Ranges and orders:** Show weapon-specific coverage rather than a single misleading all-purpose attack ring. Validate explicit attack orders against an appropriate channel. Automatic defensive engagement should not silently replace a player movement destination.
- **Naval return:** Give vessels an explicit return action to a reachable friendly naval base, using the existing water-route logic. Any automatic repair/rearm return threshold, replenishment speed, home-base selection and fallback behavior are our implementation choices. The reviewed diaries mention resupply ships but do not specify a naval automatic-return algorithm.
- **Geography:** Build on the project's attributed Natural Earth geometry and public-domain elevation assets. Use restrained ocean, coastline, terrain and relief variation so geography remains recognizable underneath faction coloring. Elevation is visual reference data, not a certified movement DEM.
- **Raised settlements:** Produce original procedural building groups and simple landmark silhouettes. Population can influence scale and density within limits; zoom should govern visibility. These shapes are illustrative and do not claim accurate individual city skylines or copied reference-game landmarks.
- **City inspection:** Selecting a settlement should show its name, owner, current/initial population and strategic status using existing project data. Present modern source populations as reference data for the alternate-history start, not reconstructed historical census figures. Selecting a city must remain distinguishable from selecting its region or a nearby unit.

The diaries are development descriptions from 2023–2024. They establish the above published concepts, not a complete verified specification of the current commercial build.
