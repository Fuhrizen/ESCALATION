# ESCALATION command interface: implementable design

## Findings from the current native prototype

- `src/main.cpp::positionPopup`, `regionPopup`, `unitPopup`: inspection creates 330 px floating windows at the click location. They cover targets, require travel to actions, do not preview on hover, and disappear when issuing explicit move mode. Unit information omits useful combat/movement capabilities.
- `hud`: 52 px top bar mixes identity, raw rates, pause, speed, save, projection, framing and map style in a single line. Bottom 46 px bar truncates hints to 100 characters. Narrow displays cannot maintain hierarchy.
- `allocation`: a one-sided research percentage appears separately in construction, forces and research. It shows RP/day and work/day without explaining which active tasks slow down. There is no immediately visible national tradeoff.
- `buildPanel` and `selectPoint`: catalog selection starts placement; placement calls `buildAt`, which creates an unfinished world site. This reverses the requested fabricate-then-place model. Catalog also omits Industrial district despite simulation support. Locked buttons remain clickable and explain failure only after selection.
- `researchTree`: existing cubic edges are a useful base, but nodes remain at field*198, tier*112. Mechanics use two parallel prerequisite paths (`tier-2`), while the layout visually resembles one ladder. Large header/queue consume canvas. An invisible node button immediately queues; effects/prerequisites are hidden in tooltip and every node exposes RP costs. Nodes are only 178x76, with wrapping name competing with year and state.
- `overlays`: facility symbols are letters or $, and always-on radar circles clutter the world. Range circles use position.x+2/3 rather than mechanical range and distort under globe/map projection. Hovered units only add a name, selected air/missile units get a circle, hovered facilities have no equivalent preview. Shared picking should drive inspector and overlay consistently.
- `controls`: WASD is already normalized and zoom-adjusted, mouse wheel and drag exist. Speed is 0.65 viewport widths/sec before smoothing; improve perceived response, user options and interruption behavior instead of duplicating bindings. Escape closes panels first, then opens command pause; preserve that ordering while adding a clearly visible options entry.

## Proposed spatial and interaction contract

Use a fixed right command dock, width 360 at >=1280px (320 at smaller widths), between a 60px top HUD and 58px bottom action rail. Its position stays fixed while content changes. Reserve the map viewport against the dock when open; never let hover cards alter camera bounds mid-gesture. Base typography 17px, metadata 14px, titles 22px, interactive rows >=36px, 12px padding, 8px gaps. Scale at 100/115/130%. Dark navy panels, off-white primary text, muted blue-gray secondary text; cyan command/available, amber queued/pending, green completed, coral hostile/error. State must also have icon+word so hue is never the sole signal.

Dock precedence: active mode (construction/research/forces) > retained selected entity > hover preview > command overview. Hover preview can occupy a small fixed section above the retained selection, avoiding destructive replacement of command actions. Label PREVIEW / SELECTED explicitly. Hovering neither changes selection nor camera. Click pins entity; blank click clears selection. Selected formation keeps hold/focus actions always reachable. Region/city inspect lists controlled settlements, buildings used/8, occupation and war state. Enemy dock is information-only except legitimate war action.

Top HUD: nation flag/name and peace/war; campaign year/day; centered pause/resume and 1x/2x/4x/8x; right options. Put projection, political/relief, fit and ranges in a compact view cluster. Show active research and construction summary with progress and ETA, including READY n badge. Keep raw currencies out of player-facing strings, including notices, logs, tooltips, unit fabrication and warheads.

Allocation: one two-ended horizontal slider, left Research and right Construction, with complementary percentages. Live two-task preview: `Computing centres · 18 days` and `Army base · 6 days` updates while dragging. Endpoint caption must say `Research paused` / `Construction paused`, never fake huge ETA from rate clamp. Retain rates/costs internally. Name construction's share honestly: `Construction & production` in tooltip if units and warheads share it. Do not silently advertise dedicated construction capacity when mechanics pool all jobs.

Bottom rail: Construction [B], Research [R], Forces [U], Alerts/log; separated placement/order status. Mode hints wrap rather than truncate. Essential actions have icon+label and clear active fill. No floating inspectors.

## Fabrication and deployment flow

Catalog card -> Queue building -> fabrication queue -> READY inventory -> Place -> confirm valid controlled land -> consume one inventory token -> operational world site.

Queue uses global/technology/campaign capacity checks, not region or coast. Manufacture does not create a Site, generate income, emit radar effects, or consume a region slot. Queue entry shows icon, name, progress, rounded days, and Cancel. Ready cards show counts and Place. Invalid placement keeps token, retains mode and gives exact reason. Escape/right click cancels placement without destroying inventory. Shift places another only while count remains. On final token, exit placement and show success. Coast validation must be shared by ghost and final commit; currently `buildReason` omits the `buildAt` coast test, so the green ghost can lie. Show translucent footprint, valid/invalid outline, applicable projected coverage, and exact locality status near cursor.

Persistence needs explicit fabrication jobs and ready counts. Update save format and legacy migration: existing unfinished sites remain legacy in-world construction or deterministically migrate to queue with preserved remaining progress; do not drop them or duplicate their benefits. Queue cap and inventory cap should have explicit messages. ETA uses the actual same pooled production algorithm as simulation, including existing unit/warhead/site jobs.

## Research graph

Dock research details and queue into a stable left strip (~280px), canvas in remaining area; preserve top playback HUD and close/back control. Default view near current available era, not full-century fit. Use horizontally advancing eras with eight field lanes grouped into clusters, with the two real prerequisite chains offset into sibling tracks within each lane. Node ~196x88, 48px horizontal and 24px vertical clearance. Use cubic splines from explicit ports; clip graph edges to canvas. Completed prerequisite links green, relevant selected ancestry cyan 2.5px, other links muted 1px. Canvas drag pan and wheel zoom around cursor; Fit, Current, field filters and search remain stable.

Single click selects a node and highlights ancestors/descendants; primary `Queue research` lives in its detail panel. Double click may queue available tech. Details show concrete benefit/unlocks, prerequisites as clickable chips, availability reason, doctrine bonus and estimated time. Readiness states: Locked, Available, Queued #n, Active progress, Complete. Inline icons/check/lock and border treatment reinforce words. Pending queue shares the same node icon and state as canvas. Existing simulation supports only already-complete prerequisites when queueing; do not promise automatic prerequisite queueing unless simulation implements it. Locked nodes should still be selectable for planning. Avoid claiming year-gating: years are reference eras, ahead-of-era cost appears as longer ETA, explained plainly.

## Ranges, icons and responsive camera

Central entity picking yields hoveredUnit/hoveredSite and selected ids once/frame. Draw only selected/hovered effects by default. Distinguish attack solid amber, sensing dashed cyan, movement/routes muted green. State actual mechanic names and km in dock. Project geographic circle samples and split invisible/dateline segments, instead of screen circles from one eastward point. Do not invent radar gameplay merely because a Radar facility exists.

Icons should be a shared ImDrawList geometric vocabulary, not generated images or font glyphs: factory sawtooth roof, laboratory flask, army base crossed rifles/chevrons, airbase runway+plane, shipyard anchor/dock, missile pointed rocket, radar concentric arcs, command radio/star, industry gear/block. Share 20px map symbols, 24px card icons, and 40px dock portraits, with 2px outlines and a dark backing. Infantry chevron, armour tank, artillery barrel, aircraft wings, destroyer ship, sub capsule, special forces dagger, missile upright rocket. Keep icons distinct in grayscale and at small size.

Camera target: 0.9-1.2 screen widths/sec normal, Shift 1.8x, normalized diagonals, zoom-compensated. Wheel ~1.18x per notch anchored under cursor, damping settling <=180ms, drag immediate or <=80ms. Stop pan on key release; do not accumulate drift. Options expose pan speed, wheel speed, smoothing, UI scale, selected/hover ranges. Middle drag unambiguously pans; right drag remains panning with click/drag threshold, right click remains order. Opening modal must suppress all map keyboard/mouse handlers and freeze simulation. Space is tactical pause without modal; Escape hierarchy remains mode->selection/panel->pause. Never auto-pan for hover/selection; only explicit Focus/Fit does that.

## Verification scenarios

At 1280x720 and 1920x1080: no overlapped HUD buttons, dock scroll works, minimum 36px targets, long names wrap without state collision. Queue two facilities while paused; unpause, adjust slider and compare actual progress/ETA; ready never produces map benefits; invalid coast placement does not decrement count; Shift placing consumes exactly count; save/reload active+ready preserves quantities. Locked tech remains inspectable; queued/active/completed state appears consistently; graph drag never pans map. Hover friendly/enemy unit/site shows dock+same effect; unhover clears preview, selection persists. Pause/options block map commands, pan settings apply, keyboard diagonal speed equals axial speed. Review whole-screen composition and interaction, not merely screenshots of individual panels.

## Primary-source research and adaptation

- ICBM: Escalation Dev Diary 3 (SoftWarWare/Slitherine): weapon systems and hull progression produce different strategic roles. Adapt into concrete inspector capabilities and research unlocks, rather than generic tier labels. https://www.slitherine.com/news/icbm-escalation-dev-diary-3
- ICBM: Escalation Dev Diary 7: territories vary in production/research importance; diplomacy intel is contextual. Adapt region inspector to strategic value/control rather than a generic orders popup. https://www.slitherine.com/news/icbm-escalation-dev-diary-7
- ICBM: Escalation Dev Diary 10: mode and pacing preferences are legitimate player choices. Keep visible pause/speed and options. https://www.slitherine.com/news/icbm-escalation-dev-diary-10-playing-your-way
- Factorio FFF 238: improve contrast/font sizing, preserve technology-card identity between graph and queue, show progress in situ, standardize button affordance. Adapt to native graph cards, queue and explicit detail action. https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-238
- Factorio FFF 423: show research status/estimated time and hover previews without requiring a player to abandon context. Adapt to persistent HUD summary and fixed hover inspector. https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-423
- Beyond All Reason developer camera microblog: users disliked camera control being taken away and preferred preserved camera settings; developer added reset/animation options. Adapt by explicit focus actions and configurable motion, no automatic camera jump on inspect. https://www.beyondallreason.info/microblogs/162
- Age of Wonders 4 official designer diary 8 (turn-based strategy, not RTS): split structure/unit queues preserves development while rebuilding military. Relevant as a clarity lesson; retain ESCALATION's shared production only if UI truthfully says so. https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/age-of-wonders-4/news/age-of-wonders-4-dev-diary-8

These are adaptations and original proposed dimensions, not claims that reference games use the exact layout or fabricate-then-place mechanic.

## Additional RTS references and exact appearance targets

- EA's official Command & Conquer Remastered help explicitly documents sidebar categories and the sequence: choose structure, wait until ready, select it again, deploy on map. This is a direct precedent for the requested manufacture/deployment split, while ESCALATION's globally stored ready inventory is an intentional adaptation. The page also documents configurable right-click commands, direction-key/edge panning, and sidebar Options. https://help.ea.com/en/articles/command-and-conquer/command-and-conquer-remastered/how-to-play/
- BAR's official command reference documents left-click selection, box select, shift-add, right-click moves and shift command queues. Adapt those basic selection conventions; keep ESCALATION's existing right-drag pan because adopting BAR's right-drag formation simultaneously would conflict. https://www.beyondallreason.info/commands-20
- Zero-K's own newcomer manual covers tactical roles, selection and queued building commands. Its player-facing unit role explanations reinforce that icons and inspector content should answer what a selected unit is useful for, not merely show a type string. https://zero-k.info/mediawiki/Newbie_Guide

Palette targets (sRGB): map-facing backing #0B1420, panel #111F2C, raised card #1A2D3B, border #37566B; primary #EDF5F7, secondary #B4C8D3, quiet #8EA7B6; available/command #63D8CF, queued #F1BB67, completed #8AD19B, hostile #F18A7D. Subdued text must stay legible against actual backing, not against the brightest atlas patch. Keep 1px dividers, 5px radius, 2px selection outlines, no dense glow or decorative chrome. Map labels use dark 1-2px shadow/backing. Pending progress uses 6px rails; health uses 4px rail plus numeric strength in dock.

Use a proportionally spaced sans face with clear numeral differentiation; source already available licensed font from assets or system font package rather than fetching an unverified remote font. DejaVu Sans or Liberation Sans is a practical native fallback. Use ordinary case for sentences and small caps only for short section labels. Do not enlarge all labels with SetWindowFontScale independently; one UI-scale setting should drive layout and fonts together.

Information pacing: immediate visual hover feedback (<one frame), optional extended tooltip after ~250ms, actionable selection immediate, notices 3-5 seconds and mirrored in log, completion badge persistent until inventory used or event reviewed. No modal for normal queue/place/hold actions. Keep the strategic nuclear release confirmation because it ends the campaign. During a paused campaign, show PAUSED prominently while keeping planning commands available. Tooltips can elaborate; basic role, blocked reason and ETA belong in visible panels.

Inspector exact first-screen hierarchy: entity icon+name / owner+status, strength or operational state, role sentence, 2x2 capability grid (movement, attack, sensing/support, applicable bonus), primary actions, queue or settlement detail. Building role and unlocks should precede optional unit production catalog. Formation multi-select should display count and composition; do not imply the first selected unit's stats apply to every selected unit. Preview content remains informational and cannot redirect the retained selected formation's actions.
