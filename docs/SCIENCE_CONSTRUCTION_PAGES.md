# Fullscreen science and construction

The supplied ICBM screenshots informed a shared fullscreen management workspace. Research and Construction tabs switch directly between pages; Exit returns to the map and Pause/Resume remains available. The national budget stays in the top HUD. Map ready rails, inspectors and lower-right navigation do not overlap these pages, and hidden map overlays are not built while a management page is open.

Research shows every implemented technology, including locked future branches. Categories organize the complete graph; All technologies includes every node and cross-category dependency. Search considers locked names too. Locked cards can be inspected and list missing prerequisites, but research queue eligibility remains governed by the simulation. This is the implemented game catalogue, not a claim that every original ICBM technology has already been implemented.

Use Fit, Current and +/- controls, Ctrl-wheel zoom, wheel/Shift-wheel scroll and right/middle drag pan. Zoom spans 0.12–2.5. Small overview nodes use tooltips and zoom for readable details. Switching to Construction and back preserves zoom and pan.

Construction uses always-visible stock and production queues, a selected-item detail card, a circular catalogue and category selection. Operational producer selection is explicit for formations. Queueing fabricates into stock; Place/Deploy is a separate map action. Research, producer and coastal restrictions still apply. Unsupported aircraft and other future systems are not presented as working production choices.

Native input regression coverage verifies locked previews, all-node membership, eligibility remaining locked, direct page switching, preserved graph navigation, category selection and view-only production without manufacturing side effects. Rendered pages were inspected at 1280×720 and 1920×1080; GitHub Actions runs the full Linux/Windows checks.
